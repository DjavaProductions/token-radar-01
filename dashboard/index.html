Methodology — model radar-2

This radar ranks every pre-token DeFi protocol by the implied market cap the market would assign it at token launch, using only the sealed DefiLlama snapshot. The model has four factors:

1. Category peer multiple

For each category in the snapshot, we compute the median(mcap / TVL) across all live-token protocols in that category (excluding CEX, bridges, liquid staking, and protocols with negative or zero mcap). This gives a data-driven multiple anchored in what the market actually pays per TVL dollar in each sector — rather than a single flat multiple for all protocols.

2. Chain diversity factor

Protocols deployed on more chains are more likely to have a broad user base. We apply a multiplier based on the number of chains:

  • 1–2 chains → ×1.00 (no bonus)
  • 3–4 chains → ×1.15
  • 5–7 chains → ×1.25
  • 8+ chains → ×1.35

3. Growth signal (7-day TVL change)

A positive 7-day TVL growth indicates rising demand. We clamp the change to [−60%, +120%] and apply a weight of 0.40, giving a growth multiplier of 1 + (clamped_change / 100) × 0.40.

4. Maturity curve

Older protocols have had more time to build user trust and liquidity. The maturity multiplier rises from 0.60 (brand new) to 1.00 at ≥ 3 years old: 0.60 + min(age_days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.40.

Implied value formula

implied = max(1, round(
  tvl_usd
  × peer_multiple(category)
  × chain_diversity_mult(chains)
  × growth_mult(change_7d)
  × maturity_mult(age_days)
))

Farm plans

Each protocol gets a 7-step farm plan derived from its category, TVL tier, age, and primary chain. The plan tells you: how much capital to deploy, the primary on-chain action to register as an active user, how often to interact, how long to hold, and how to claim — all without relying on any information outside the sealed snapshot.

Board coverage

The board always covers every watchlist slug (40 protocols at or above $50M TVL with no token). It also includes the top 180 pre-token protocols below the floor, ranked by TVL — the early radar finds value before it reaches the floor.
